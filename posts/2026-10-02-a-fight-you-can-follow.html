See the contact, then the recoil

The approved mineral orbs and balanced scene lighting remain in the authored reference fight. At the first contact, the camera now stays close through two graphic frames and four held frames, then opens continuously during recoil. This revision changes only that camera passage around 3.2 seconds; the body paths, native mechanics and holds are unchanged.

Both whole shells are visible in the held contact pose. At the dive reveal, BLAZE again carries a slim ember blade with a plasma layer, without the earlier dark disc across its face; the blade material still needs work. The landing keeps both full silhouettes visible with contact-timed compression. These selected pauses, including hitstop, cannot establish fluidity by themselves.

Gemini describes the revised framing as more fluid and powerful and prefers it to the previous camera pass. The full ten-second film remains private: linear transitions and hovering, partial guard and foreground occlusion, pale flat bright cores and the balance between materials and action still need work. Poster and share quality remain unfinished, the full frame review has not passed, and the reference-fight milestone is not approved.
