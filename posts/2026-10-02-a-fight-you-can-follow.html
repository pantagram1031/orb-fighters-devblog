Letting the landing read

The approved mineral orbs are now in the reference film. Balanced scene lighting restores warm TERRA and red BLAZE without changing their approved material maps. Fine wear, cream glyphs and textured inlays now appear in the actual fight scene.

A grounded lateral feint and a clear contact-timed landing improve the scene. The selected pauses show the adopted appearances and contact pose, but they cannot establish the quality of continuous motion.

Bright cores remain pale and flat. Earlier exchanges still have occlusion, weak weight and pivoting, and apparent attack or debris clipping. Motion and frame reviews still reject the ten-second film, which remains private; the reference-fight milestone is not approved.
