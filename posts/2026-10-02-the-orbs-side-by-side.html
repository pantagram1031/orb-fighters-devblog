Original small glyphs, eight shell directions

The eight orbs keep fine stone and ceramic mineral wear, distinct shell colours and their original small cream eye glyphs. MAGNUS stays dark slate. These paired views show the selected paintings beside the latest appearance in a dark studio.

Core interiors still look flat, halo hue is subtle, and the bright currents can look grainy. These stills cannot demonstrate moving currents; that needs a moving capture. Appearance adoption does not finish the motion or gameplay work.
