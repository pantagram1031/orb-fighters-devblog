Original small glyphs, eight shell directions

The eight shells now use fine mineral pores, inclusions and uneven wear instead of broad cloudy patches. Stone and ceramic stay restrained, while wider coloured channels carry texture in a dark studio. MAGNUS keeps its dark slate body.
