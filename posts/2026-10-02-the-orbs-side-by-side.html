02 Oct 2026, 16:05 KSTWork in progress

Original small glyphs, eight shell directions

Eight orb shells now read more like smooth stone and ceramic. Wider lit inlays stand out in a darker studio, while the small original eye glyphs and distinct colours remain.

These paired views place the selected paintings beside the latest renders. TERRA has a smoother warm shell and a broad green inlay. MAGNUS keeps its dark slate body and blue meridian; the busy surface patterns have been pared back.

The painting match is still work in progress. Shell wear looks cloudy and cleaner than the paintings, and the eyes have fairly uniform bright cores. The next pass needs more convincing wear and depth in the light without losing the simple glyphs.
