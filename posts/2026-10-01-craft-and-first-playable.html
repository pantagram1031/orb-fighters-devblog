A clearer fight, a brighter world

Orb Fighters is building toward fluid, creative power fights in a world that remembers what happened. The first reference spar pairs BLAZE's plasma with TERRA's stone shaping. That short fight sets the quality target before the systems grow around it.

Our latest review found that some exchanges hide or crop a fighter. We are refining the camera, eye acting and effect staging so the cause and consequence read at a glance. The visual direction is bright and colourful, with realistic materials and bold, clean power shapes.

Our review process pairs short renders with structured checks. Alongside motion and material checks, we look at thumbnail readability, focal brightness, silhouettes, paused compositions and whether a moment feels worth sharing. The gallery will show films only after they pass that review.

The first playable target is TERRA against an AI BLAZE on the Duel Square. It will bring together gesture controls, quick reactions, a third-person lock-on camera and persistent building with Clean and Keep. The reference fight is still in progress; no milestone approval or playable release is being announced today.
