A cleaner reference fight

The latest reference spar keeps the clearer blade strike and the warm stone and vivid red of TERRA and BLAZE. The plasma strand now has a curl instead of reading as a straight light stripe. This is a small improvement in the fight's shape language, with more work still needed to make the blade feel hot and flowing.

We cut back the steam, sparks and speed lines that crowded the action. The matched frames below show the same contact before and after that pass. The quieter version gives the fighters and the blade more room to read, while preserving the original contact and timing.

The film is still a work in progress. BLAZE has a counter reaction, but the shot looks away from its face at the wrong moment. Stone guards also hide part of the exchange, and the blade's connection to the body needs to stay clear before contact. Those are the next corrections.

A short-preview tool is prepared so we can test one weak beat at a time before rendering the whole fight again. The clip here is authored reference animation, not a player or AI gameplay demonstration. Hands-on play still needs a reliable visible game window. M1 remains in progress, and the Gallery has no approved fight film yet.
